AI Influencer — Avatar Source

When a brand creates their AI Influencer, what image should the system work from?

A

Upload a real photo

Brand uploads a photo of their actual brand ambassador or spokesperson. The AI animates that specific person's face/likeness to deliver the product review — so the "influencer" looks like a real, known person from the brand.

Best for: brands with an existing ambassador they want to use without filming

B

Design a virtual influencer from scratch

Brand describes or customizes a fully AI-generated virtual persona — choosing appearance, style, and personality. No real person involved. The AI creates a consistent virtual character that becomes the brand's "digital influencer".

Best for: brands who want a unique digital mascot with no IP concerns

C

Both — brand chooses per video

Support both approaches. Brand can have a library of "AI Influencers" — some based on uploaded photos, some fully virtual. They pick which influencer appears in each video.

Best for: flexibility and future-proofing
